The signal path

A livestream, reflected onto Nostr.

Paste a YouTube or Twitch URL. The video is pulled, transcoded, and re-served as HLS, then announced to Nostr as a NIP-53 live event any client (zap.stream, Amethyst) can tune into.

Self-host

One box. One command. Your keys.

The app, MediaMTX, ffmpeg, and yt-dlp ship in a single image. No cloud, no account — home-hosting also sidesteps the IP blocks that break cloud streaming.

# clone, then:
docker compose up -d        # → http://localhost:3000

# public/LAN — point viewers at your node:
HLS_BASE=http://your-host:3000/live docker compose up -d
HLS_BASEpublic URL the Nostr event links to (defaults to localhost)
NOSTR_RELAYScomma-separated relays to broadcast to (sane defaults)
PORTapp port (default 3000)

Identity & zaps

Every broadcast is zappable.

⚡ Lightning out of the box

Each stream carries a <npub>@npub.cash Lightning address. Viewers zap the live event; the sats land as ecash at npub.cash.

To claim: sign in to npub.cash with the stream's nsec (shown in the app when you go live) and withdraw. Persistent / bring-your-own identity is on the roadmap — today, copy the nsec to keep the funds.

Scope

A lean mirror, on purpose.

One clean path — yt-dlp → ffmpeg → MediaMTX — built for self-hosting from home. Open source, MIT. Mirror only content you have the right to redistribute.
